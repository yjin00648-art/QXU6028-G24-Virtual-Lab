# L2 AI Usage Record

- **Phase:** Regular L2 DoS inversion and refined-gap checkpoint (original log dated 23 September 2026).
- **Record consolidated:** 28 October 2026 (reviewed by Jin Zhengyang).
- **Tool:** OpenAI Codex (as stated in `AI_usage_log_L2_original.txt`).

## Purpose and instruction summary

We used AI assistance to implement the published carrier-density integrals, signed independent normalisation of RH and Rxx, 400-point trial Fermi-level grid, joint nearest-curve residual, 15% model-selection rule and 200-replicate paired resampling. For the September checkpoint, we also ran a refined gap search around the minimum to compare with the prescribed 0.25 kBT grid.

## Output adopted and subsequent audit

In the September checkpoint archive (`G24_L2_Checkpoint.zip`), the refined search gave Eg/kBT = 2.0730 with 16th–84th percentile interval [2.00, 2.13] kBT (compared with 2.00 kBT on the prescribed 0.25 kBT grid). During our October–November audit, we identified two implementation defects in the legacy optimiser (E01 fixed narrow search window; E02 missing cone return branch) and corrected both in the shared `g24lab/l2.py` module without altering the preserved historical checkpoint files. For the final regular submission (`results.json`), we report the prescribed 0.25 kBT grid outcome (2.00 kBT) and cross-check it against the refined checkpoint (2.0730 kBT).

## Checks and retained evidence

- Prescribed 0.25 kBT grid (final regular `results.json`): `hard_gap`, Eg/kBT = 2.00, RMS = 0.01145077 vs cone RMS = 0.06414722 (82.15% reduction), 200/200 hard-gap bootstrap selections (169 at 2.00, 31 at 2.25; p16–p84 [2.00, 2.00] kBT).
- Refined September checkpoint: `hard_gap`, Eg/kBT = 2.0730, RMS = 0.01080589 (83.15% reduction), median 2.06 kBT, p16–p84 [2.00, 2.13] kBT, 58 assigned Fermi levels.
- All files in `G24_L2_Checkpoint.zip` match `L2/recorded/` byte-for-byte.
- The final regular Fermi level increases overall from -0.0890 eV to +0.1401 eV. It decreases from +0.02449 eV to +0.02339 eV at zero-based indices 34 and 35. [Regular L2 trajectory](../../validation/final_2026-11-10/L2_calculations.csv)
