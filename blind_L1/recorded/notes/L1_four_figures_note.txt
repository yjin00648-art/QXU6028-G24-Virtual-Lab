G24 supplementary carrier-transport figures
Model: hard_gap, Eg/kBT = 3.531, T = 300.0 K
L1 operating point marked at EF = 0.1235779450 eV

The electron-like and hole-like populations use the course Fermi-window integrals with the G24 density-of-states scale. The Hall coefficient and sheet resistance follow RH=(p-n)/(p+n)^2 and Rs=1/(p+n), then each curve is normalised according to the L2 method. Measured repeat means are projected to their nearest points on the joint RH-Rs model curve.

These four plots are supplementary. They do not replace the official L1 factor-sweep figure C(EF), which remains in the checkpoint.
